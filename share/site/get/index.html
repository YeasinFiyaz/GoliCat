What the cat does

  • Walks when you walk, and races with its scarf flying when you're in a rickshaw, bus or car.
  • Takes a cha break when you stop for a while.
  • Stuck in traffic? When your bus or rickshaw doesn't move for 20 seconds, it puts on headphones and listens to a song.
  • Goes to work: set your office and it sits at a desk, typing, after you've been there 2 minutes.
  • Does homework at your school or university, glasses on, pencil in paw.
  • Puts on headphones and vibes to the music at your hangout spot.
  • Sleeps under a nakshi kantha when you're home.
  • Choose your cat: Goli and ten more colours, plus six hero cats. Friends see the same one in your live link.
  • Pulls out a paper map when GPS is weak.
  • Tap the cat to play. When the trip ends you get a 3-panel comic of it, with no addresses in it.
  • Live sharing: send a link and friends watch your cat move in their browser. It stops when you say, or when the trip ends.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the downloaded GoliCat file.
  2. If your phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager.
  3. If you see "App scan recommended" from Google Play Protect, tap Scan app. Google checks GoliCat in a few seconds, then tap Install. Every new app that isn't from the Play Store gets this check. It doesn't mean anything is wrong.
  4. Open GoliCat, tap Start roaming, and allow location while using the app and notifications. The notification shows while the cat is roaming with you, even when the app is closed.
  5. On Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco, Oppo, Vivo or Realme phones, also turn on Autostart and set Battery saver → No restrictions for GoliCat in the phone's settings. Otherwise the phone may stop the cat when the screen is off.

    Your privacy

    Your location, office and home stay on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or trackers. Only while you are live sharing does your latest position go to the GoliCat server, and it's deleted when sharing stops. Read the privacy policy.
