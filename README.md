# GoliCat · গলির বিড়াল

<img src="branding/logo_d_512.png" alt="GoliCat logo" width="120" align="right">

**A Dhaka street cat that walks your streets with you.**

GoliCat shows your real location on the map as an animated cat with a red scarf. It walks
when you walk, races when you're in a rickshaw, bus or car, takes a cha break when you stop,
types at a desk when you reach your office and naps under a nakshi kantha when you get home.

Free for Android. No ads, no account.

## Install

1. Open the [download page](https://golicat.yeasinfiyaz.workers.dev/get/) on your phone and tap
   **Download for Android**, then open the downloaded GoliCat file.
2. If your phone asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
3. If Google Play Protect says **"App scan recommended"**, tap **Scan app**, wait a few seconds,
   then tap **Install**. Every app from outside the Play Store gets this check. It doesn't mean
   anything is wrong.
4. On Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco, Oppo, Vivo or Realme phones, turn on **Autostart** for GoliCat and set
   **Battery saver → No restrictions** in the phone's settings. Otherwise the phone may stop the
   cat when the screen is off.

Older or very cheap phone and the app won't install? The download page has a second version for
older phones.

## Getting started

**Go for a real walk.**

1. Tap **Start roaming**.
2. Allow location **while using the app** and allow **notifications**.
3. Put your phone away. The cat follows you on the map, and a "GoliCat is roaming with you"
   notification stays while the trip runs, even if you close the app or lock the screen.
4. When you're done, open GoliCat and tap **End trip**.

**Set your places.** Long-press the map where a place is and choose what it is: **office**,
**home**, **school / university** or **hangout spot**. You can also use **Use my current
location** and adjust any of them later in **Places & settings**. After about two minutes at a
place the cat settles in: at the office it sits down at its desk and types; at school or
university it puts on its glasses and does homework; at your hangout spot it puts on
headphones and vibes to the music; at home it goes to sleep.

**Get your trip comic.** Every trip ends with a three-panel comic of the best moments. You can
save it to your gallery or share it. It never shows a map, route or address.

**Stuck in traffic.** If your bus or rickshaw doesn't move for 20 seconds, the cat puts on its
headphones and listens to a song until you're moving again.

**Play with the cat.** Tap it. It does something different in each mode.

**Choose your cat.** Tap the face button next to **Start roaming** (or **Choose your cat** in
**Places & settings**). There are 17 cats: Goli and ten more colours, each with its own name, plus
six hero cats. Your choice is saved on your phone and used everywhere: on the map, in your comics and
in live-share links, so friends see the same cat you do.

## Share your live location

Like live location sharing in Google Maps, but your friends see a cat.

1. During a trip, tap the share-location button.
2. Choose how long: **15 min**, **1 hour**, **4 hours** or **Until trip ends**.
3. Send the link. Anyone with it can watch your cat move in their web browser, with no app needed.

Sharing stops when you tap **Stop sharing**, when the time runs out or when you end the trip.
Your position is then deleted from the server.

## Privacy

- Your office, home, settings and trips are stored **only on your phone**.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.
- Your position leaves the phone only while you are live sharing, and only the latest one is kept.

Read the full [privacy policy](https://golicat.yeasinfiyaz.workers.dev/privacy/) (English and Bangla).

## Questions

**The cat stops when my screen is off.** Turn on Autostart and set Battery saver to
No restrictions for GoliCat (install step 4). Some phones close apps in the background otherwise.

**The cat isn't moving.** GPS needs a clear view of the sky. Indoors or between tall buildings
the cat pulls out a paper map until it finds you again.

**Is it on the Play Store?** Not yet. For now, download it from the
[download page](https://golicat.yeasinfiyaz.workers.dev/get/).
